Delete your account and data

You can request deletion of your Vagus account and all associated data at any time by following the steps below.

How to request deletion
1
Send an email to the Vagus developer

Use the address and subject line shown below. Include the email address linked to your Vagus account so we can locate it.

To justanappearance@gmail.com Subject Account Deletion Request — Vagus
2
Confirmation within 30 days

You'll receive a reply confirming your account has been deleted. Most requests are completed within a few days.

3
Uninstall the app

After your account is deleted server-side, uninstalling Vagus removes all locally stored data (artists, tags, listening history) from your device.
