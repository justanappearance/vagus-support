Vagus

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 2, 2026

Overview

Vagus is a music release tracker. You follow artists, and the app notifies you when they release new music. This policy explains what data the app collects to make that work, who it's shared with, and how long it's kept.

Vagus is developed and operated by a single developer. Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

What we collect

Data Why it's collected
Email address & account info To identify your account and sync your data across devices. Provided via Google Sign-In or email/password through Firebase Authentication.
Artists you track Stored in Firestore to sync your library across devices and power the release check pipeline.
Tags and tiers Labels you assign to artists, stored in your account to filter and organize your library.
Inbox and release history Releases added to your inbox, including album name, release date, and artist. Stored per-user in Firestore.
Spotify access token A temporary OAuth token used to search for and identify artists on Spotify. Vagus does not access your listening history, playlists, or playback controls.
Push notification token A device token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging, used to deliver new release notifications. No messages are sent without your involvement in setting up tracking.
Subscription status Purchase receipts and subscription state are managed by RevenueCat to verify access to premium features. RevenueCat receives your device's purchase receipt; Vagus does not store payment card details.
Last.fm API key (optional) If you choose to enter a Last.fm key for scrobbling, it is stored locally on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Local cache

Vagus stores a local SQLite cache on your device containing your artist list, release catalogue, and inbox — so the app works without waiting on a network request. This data mirrors what's in your Firestore account and is cleared when you sign out.

What we don't collect

  • Listening history or playback data
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Microphone or camera access
  • Data from users who are not signed in

Third-party services

Vagus uses the following third-party services to function. Each service's privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Firebase (Google)

Used for account authentication (Firebase Auth), data storage (Firestore), and push notifications (Cloud Messaging). Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Spotify

Used to search for and identify artists. Vagus requests read-only access and does not read your Spotify library, listening history, or control playback. Governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.

Deezer

Used to fetch release and catalogue data for artists. No user account or login is required. Governed by Deezer's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

Used to manage in-app subscriptions and verify purchase receipts. Governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Last.fm (optional)

If you choose to enable Last.fm integration, track listens may be scrobbled to your Last.fm account. This is opt-in and requires you to supply your own API key. Governed by Last.fm's Privacy Policy.

Data retention & deletion

Your Firestore data (artists, inbox, tags) is retained as long as your account exists. The local device cache is cleared when you sign out.

To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below. We will remove your Firestore data within 30 days.

Children

Vagus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
